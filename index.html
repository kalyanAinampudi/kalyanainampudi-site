About Me

Hi, I’m Kalyan Ainampudi.

I’m a Computer Science and Engineering student from India, currently exploring the world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. But I don’t like defining myself only by what I study.

I’m naturally curious about a lot of things—technology, investing, philosophy, psychology, history, fitness, travel, and the future. I enjoy going down rabbit holes, questioning how things work, and learning about subjects that are completely outside my usual field.

I like building things and experimenting with ideas. Sometimes that means writing code and exploring AI. Other times it means thinking about money, careers, entrepreneurship, or what life might look like ten or twenty years from now.

I’m also interested in understanding people and the ideas that have shaped civilization. I read philosophy and classical works, explore different schools of thought, and enjoy thinking about questions that don't always have simple answers—What makes a good life? What does freedom really mean? How should we use wealth? What will technology do to humanity?

Outside the digital world, I enjoy running, cycling, endurance sports, travelling, and exploring new experiences. I’m drawn toward things that challenge me physically and mentally rather than simply being comfortable.

I’m still figuring things out, and that is part of the point.

This blog is a place for me to think out loud, document what I learn, share things I find interesting, and keep track of my journey. Some posts may be about technology. Others might be about books, money, philosophy, travel, fitness, or simply an idea that caught my attention.

I don't expect to have all the answers.

I just want to keep learning, building, questioning, and experiencing more of the world.

This is my journey, documented along the way.
